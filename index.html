  1. May 2025 —
    Present

    Founder

    DDX Consulting · ddx-consulting.com · alongside Gategroup

    DDX is a consultancy that helps clients run their operations better with AI. The work comes in two stages, and each has its own DDX product. Graham understands the company. Tally does the work. DDX sits between them as the consultant. Delivered for 50+ clients.

    Stage 1 · Understand · in build

    Graham

    Graham is how DDX works out how a client's company really runs, where the time goes and where it hurts. It has its own login and its own database, separate from Tally.

    1. Company Brain

      Clients upload documents and the AI reads, sorts and files them. Scans and images are on the way. A separate Interview Brain holds every interview. DDX sees all of it. Clients see only the verified results, never which employee said what.

    2. AI interviews

      DDX picks the company and the people, and staff get a link with no account to create. The AI works from a brief and asks follow-up questions instead of running a fixed questionnaire. It finds the real process, where the time goes and what hurts. Text today, a voice version later.

    3. Reports

      The AI pulls findings, contradictions and gaps from the interviews and documents into a first draft, with every point sourced, and marks where an AI agent would fit. DDX reviews it, then hands it over. The AI never sends anything to a client itself.

    4. Ask Graham

      Clients ask questions and get answers from their own Brain, with citations. They can also connect their own Claude or ChatGPT to it over MCP.

    Each company's data stays fully separate. Files and database sit in London, AI processing runs in the EU, and DDX operators need 2FA on top of client SSO. Built so far: login, the operator console, document upload, text interviews and EU processing. In progress: Brain search, interview setup and reports.

    Stage 2 · Do · next after Graham

    Tally

    Tally is the AI operations platform for automation and agents. From the opportunities Graham finds, it builds a team of agents that do the work for the client: handling processes, replying, tidying data. Clients mostly run it by chatting. Brain and interviews have moved out to Graham, so Tally now does automation only.

    1. Understand. DDX interviews the client's staff in Graham and feeds in their documents.
    2. Report. Graham shows where the problems are and which few places suit an agent best.
    3. Build. DDX agrees the plan with the client and builds the agents in Tally.
    4. Run. Tally's agents read Graham's Brain through an integration, so they already know the company's data and processes.
    5. Measure. More interviews in Graham show what improved and where to go next.
